
\subsection{Error-Sensitivity Validation: CIFAR-10N}
\label{sec:cifar10n}

CIFAR-10N includes human-annotated noise labels to the CIFAR-10 training set, 
capturing the disagreements of three independent annotators per image with the 
original clean label. To validate the Gaussian-thresholding mechanism in 
Section~\ref{sec:gaussian}, we can count how many annotators disagreed with 
the clean label for each image (0 to 3). This can be done without a separate 
deployment sample, as both models are evaluated on the same $50,000$ training 
images scored under Equations~\ref{eq:conf} and \ref{eq:margin}.

\subsubsection{Model Adaptation Task: Landscape Recognition and ARXPHOTO314}
\label{sec:target_dataset}
 


To assess annotation-budget prioritization during a domain shift (\textbf{H2}), 
we utilize the Landscape Recognition dataset \cite{utkarsh} as the training set 
and a subset of ARXPHOTO314 \cite{arxnetsa:2025} as the deployment set.

The Landscape Recognition collection includes handpicked web-scraped photos 
from five categories (Coast, Desert, Forest, Glacier, and Mountain) totaling over 
$12,000$. This is the reference distribution used to train both models.

The ARXPHOTO314 dataset contains $18,320$ unrestricted real-world 
mobile pictures with high variability in lighting, framing, and 
environmental circumstances. Five top-level categories are directly 
mapped to Landscape Recognition classes (Table~\ref{tab:classmap}), but other 
categories (e.g., People, Food, Art, Architecture) are excluded. 
Section~\ref{sec:res_finetune} selects annotation budgets from this target pool, 
which indicates a real-world domain shift compared to the curated training pictures.
